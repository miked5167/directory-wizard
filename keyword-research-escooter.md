## KEY MARKET INSIGHT

**CRITICAL FINDING**: Electric scooter search volume is **significantly LARGER** than e-bikes:
- "Electric scooter" = 150,000-200,000 monthly searches
- "Electric bike" = 90,000-120,000 monthly searches
- **E-scooters are 30-40% larger market by search volume**

Additionally:
- Google Trends shows e-scooter searches surged from 73 (Jun 2024) to 100 (Nov 2024)

- USA = 85% of North American market share

## Search Volume Estimation Method

**US Market Multiplier**: US search volumes are typically **4-5x** UK volumes for consumer products
- Example: UK keyword with 1,000 searches/month ≈ 4,000-5,000 US searches/month

**Difficulty Assessment Scale** (based on competitive analysis):
- **Low (1-30)**: Few established sites, content opportunities
- **Medium (31-60)**: Mix of established and newer sites, requires quality content
- **High (61-80)**: Major publishers, requires strong authority
- **Very High (81-100)**: Dominated by brands, e-commerce giants, major publications
