## Search Volume Estimation Method

**US Market Multiplier**: US search volumes are typically **4-5x** UK volumes for consumer products
- Example: UK keyword with 1,000 searches/month ≈ 4,000-5,000 US searches/month

**Difficulty Assessment Scale** (based on competitive analysis):
- **Low (1-30)**: Few established sites, content opportunities
- **Medium (31-60)**: Mix of established and newer sites, requires quality content
- **High (61-80)**: Major publishers, requires strong authority
- **Very High (81-100)**: Dominated by brands, e-commerce giants, major publications

## COMPETITOR KEYWORD PERFORMANCE INSIGHTS

### Highly Competitive SERPs (Avoid unless you have strong DA)

- **"electric bike"** → Amazon, Walmart, Trek, REI, Best Buy dominating
- **"best electric bike"** → Consumer Reports, Bicycling.com, Tom's Guide, OutdoorGearLab
- **"cheap electric bike"** → Major retailers + deal sites

### Moderate Competition (Winnable with quality content)

- **Price range queries** ("under $1000", "under $2000")
- **Category specific** ("cargo bike", "commuter bike")
- **Brand comparisons** ("[Brand A] vs [Brand B]")
- **Reviews** for specific models

### Low Competition (Quick Wins)

- **State-specific laws** (massive opportunity across all 50 states)
- **Insurance queries** (severely underserved)
- **Local service searches** ("repair near me", "shop in [city]")
- **Technical specs** ("500 watt", "class 2", "pedal assist")
- **Long-tail feature queries** ("for hills", "with throttle", "for seniors")
